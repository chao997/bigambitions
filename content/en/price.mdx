Big Ambitions costs $22.99 on the US Steam store as of September 2026, but almost nobody should pay that number. The game discounts regularly: the 1.0 launch window ran a 20% promotion (about $20.79), and the all-time low across official stores is around $17.24 — a 25% discount recorded in July 2024. This page tracks the price picture, how regional pricing changes the math, and how to know when a deal is real.

All figures here were checked in September 2026 against SteamDB's price tracker and deal aggregators like gg.deals. Prices move with sales, and Big Ambitions has just left Early Access, so expect this page to be revised after each sale season.

## Current price and recent history

The US dollar price has been stable at $22.99 for the 1.0 release, which is the price the full version carries outside promotions. During the August 28, 2026 launch window the store ran a 20% discount, bringing it to roughly $20.79 — a fairly typical indie gesture for the week of a big release. If you are reading this during a sale banner, that is the price to compare everything else against.

The historical floor matters for patience planning. SteamDB's lowest-recorded price is about $17.24 (-25%) from July 2024, back when the game was still in Early Access. It is reasonable to expect similar or deeper cuts in future seasonal sales now that 1.0 is out, but note that the game has held its value unusually well for an indie sim — deep 50%-style discounts have not been part of its history so far.

## Regional pricing

Steam applies regional pricing, so the answer to "how much does Big Ambitions cost" depends on your account country. Major markets price around the US dollar figure — European pricing has hovered near the equivalent of €20-23, and UK pricing near £17-19 — while Steam's regional price tiers set lower absolute prices in parts of Latin America, Asia and Türkiye. SteamDB maintains the full per-region table, which is the only source worth quoting because it updates whenever the publisher adjusts a region.

Two practical notes. Regional prices move independently of US sales, so a region can be discounted while the US store is full price. And buying gifts or keys from other regions to dodge your own pricing violates Steam's rules and risks the gift or key being voided — the small saving is not worth a banned purchase.

## Sales pattern and when to buy

Big Ambitions has followed the standard Steam sale calendar: Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter sales, plus publisher-driven promotions around content releases like the 1.0 launch. Discounts so far have topped out at about 25% off. Given the 1.0 release just landed in August 2026, the nearest likely windows are the Autumn and Winter sales, and the safest prediction is another 20-25% band rather than a record low.

If you want it now and it is not on sale, remember the third-party market from our [Steam key guide](/steam-key): authorized resellers and marketplaces sometimes sit a little under Steam's sticker, and gg.deals will show you both against the official-store price. Anything advertised far below the ~$17 historical floor is a red flag, not a bargain.

## Is it worth the asking price?

Price is only half the question; content per dollar is the other half. For $22.99 you get a completed, well-reviewed management sim that received free content updates for three and a half years before 1.0 — cinema and theater businesses, the blueprint editor, factories, Steam Workshop support and more all arrived post-purchase during Early Access at no extra cost. Measured in hours per dollar, community playtime on this genre routinely runs into the hundreds.

Whether you should pay full price on day one of your purchase depends on your patience and the sale calendar above. The game has never had a deep discount, so waiting for a half-price sale may be waiting for something that never comes. If the current figure stings, the practical compromise is wishlisting now and letting the next seasonal sale decide — see our [Steam key](/steam-key) page if you would rather compare third-party offers in the meantime.

The $22.99 purchase includes the complete 1.0 game: the full New York City map, 20+ business types, the warehouse and factory logistics chains, The Hamptons added in the August 2026 release, Steam Workshop support on Windows and macOS, and over three years of free content updates that Hovgaard Games shipped through Early Access. ## How this page tracks prices

The numbers here come from two independent sources cross-checked against each other: SteamDB, which records every price change and discount in the game's history, and deal aggregators like gg.deals, which compare the official store against key marketplaces. When the two agree, the figure goes on this page with its date. That is also why we do not print a price without one — store prices change without notice, and a stale "current price" is worse than a dated one. Check back after sale seasons, or use the official Discord and Steam news to catch promotional announcements the moment they post.

## Payment and gifting notes

Purchases go through Steam's normal checkout: wallet balance, cards or the regional payment methods your store supports, and the game can be bought as a gift for another Steam account if you are pulling a friend into the city with you. What you cannot do is transfer an activated key or a purchase between accounts afterward, so decide between "gift" and "buy for me" before checkout rather than after. And if a discount is active, the price shown at checkout is per-copy — gifting three friends means three copies at the sale price, which is still the cheapest group rate Big Ambitions will ever have.
