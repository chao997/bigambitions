Big Ambitions Wiki is a static, fan-made reference site. This policy explains what information the site collects, what it does not collect, and what happens when you follow links to other websites. It applies to this website only.

## What we collect

The site itself collects no personal information. There are no accounts, no registrations, no newsletters and no comment forms, so you cannot give us data we do not want. Like nearly all web servers, our hosting provider automatically records standard technical logs — such as IP addresses, requested pages, timestamps and browser user agents — for security and operational purposes. These logs are retained only for a short operational period and are not used to build profiles of visitors.

## Cookies and local storage

The site sets no advertising or analytics cookies of its own. Your theme preference (light or dark) is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device, so the site remembers how you like it to look. This preference never leaves your browser and can be erased at any time by clearing your browser storage for this site.

## Third-party links and embeds

This wiki links to external websites and services, including the official Big Ambitions website, Steam, YouTube, Discord and Reddit. When you follow those links, the privacy practices of the destination apply — not this policy. In particular, embedded or linked video content on YouTube is subject to Google's privacy policy and may involve cookies set by that platform once you interact with it. We link to these platforms for reference; we do not control their data handling.

## Children

The site is a general-audience game reference and does not knowingly collect any personal information from any visitor, including children under 13. Since no personal information is collected at all, none is knowingly collected from children.

## Your rights

Because we do not collect, store or process personal data beyond short-lived server logs, there is no account data to access, correct, export or delete. If you believe we have processed personal information in error, contact us through the community channels listed in the site footer and we will address it.

## Changes to this policy

If the site's data practices ever change — for example, if analytics are introduced — this page will be updated with a new date at the top, and the change will be described here in plain language. This policy was last updated on the date shown above.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the community channels linked in the footer of this site. Big Ambitions Wiki is an unofficial fan project and is not affiliated with Hovgaard Games ApS, which owns all rights to the game Big Ambitions.
